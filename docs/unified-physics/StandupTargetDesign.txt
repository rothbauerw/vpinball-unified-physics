1. BODIES AND COORDINATES
-------------------------
a. One moving body, the face (face + rivet + the blade's share), mass 2.6-4.4 g ESTIMATED (3.5 g used on paper), on two coordinates:
   x      travel of the face centre along its rest normal (positive back toward the plate);
   theta  twist about the blade's long axis (off-centre hits), inertia ESTIMATED from the face size (1.5e-7 kg m^2 used on paper, ASSUMED until computed per target).
   CHANGED 11-14 (user: "account for when parts touch"; paper check 13, runs/2026-11-14-standup-shapes): tilt IS a coordinate. The face is a rigid body on the
   blade tip with travel, tilt and twist; the blade by beam theory, EI / L^3 [[12, -6 L], [-6 L, 4 L^2]] on (travel, tilt), G J / L on twist. With only the ball
   pushing at the centre the tilt comes out as 1.5 x / L (the old tie, now a result). The face's top edge reaches the plate first (2-11 deg), then pivots flat.
   Face mass and inertias from the drawn mesh outline (piece 1: 1.46 g for Gorgar's round faces, not the 3.5 g of the papers).
b. Supports on the face (each a potential plus a named loss):
   blade A          k_A x, k_A = 3 E I / L^3 from blade sizes (165-5100 N/m ESTIMATED); twist G J / L (0.16-2.9 N m/rad ESTIMATED).
   switch B + C     k_BC (x - x_close) past the switch's closing travel (900 N/m at the face ASSUMED); the helper blade (no plate) is a stiffer C, after closing.
   foam pad         Hunt-Crossley k_f x (1 + alpha x_dot), k_f 600 N/m TUNED, stiffening past 1 mm (K 7.88e7 N/m^2 TUNED: a 3 m/s centre hit travels about 3 mm),
                    alpha 0.373 s/m (80 % return of the foam alone at 1 m/s, TUNED); one-sided (pushes only); resists twist with its stiffness over its width (w^2 / 12).
   blade stack loss one loss tangent on blade A, B + C and twist (the shared law's form: damper c = tanDelta sqrt(k m_f), damping ratio tanDelta / 2): the
                    clamped stack and its fibre insulators (INFERRED source). 0.05 TUNED from the user's "a struck face stops within 1 s at most" (11-14): the
                    light blade's 31 Hz ring falls to 1 % in 0.94 s, the heavy blade's 223 Hz in 0.13 s, twist 0.18 s. On a hit (ball carried) it is a damping
                    ratio of about 0.005: under 1 % of the ball's energy (INFERRED, to check on paper).
   back plate       a fixed stop at x = gap (6.35 mm, fat targets 3.2 mm): the face meets it by the BodyStop impact law (bodypart.h).
c. Stiffest internal contacts and what is done with them (ModelStandards 3a): ball-face plastic contact (about 1 MN/m against a 3.5 g face: 0.34 ms period) and
   face-plate: impact laws, no stiff spring. Springs left in the motion: blade, B + C, foam; the stiffest is the densified foam with the ball on it (about 3.3 ms period).


2. BALL ON THE FACE (the shared law for a ball on a light part; also for the Button, to-do 11.1)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a. Impact: a ball meeting the free face at closing speed u along the contact normal: normal impulse (1 + e) mu u, mu = m m_eff / (m + m_eff), m_eff the face's mass
   at the contact point along the normal (travel and twist mobility: 1 / m_eff = n_x^2 / m_f + ((r x n)_z)^2 / I_theta); e(u) from the shared Hertz + Kuwabara-Kono law
   at mass mu (ImpactRestitutionKK, a table as BodyStopLaw). Friction: a Coulomb impulse, at most mu_face times the normal impulse, capped where the sliding stops.
b. Rest rule (as bodypart.h): if 1/2 mu (e u)^2 <= 2/5 F d(F), the pair joins (momentum kept; the rest of the relative energy is the loss of the bounces that would
   follow). F = mu times the face point's acceleration toward the ball from ALL its supports (travel and twist): with the travel spring alone, off-centre hits on
   the heavy blade took up to 2393 impacts (paper check 12).
c. Carried: the joined ball and face move together along the normal (section 3 gives the solver form). The contact force is solved, not taken from a dent; the shared
   friction (sideways spring capped at mu N) and twisting friction use it. Released when the contact force at a step's end reaches zero.
d. CHANGED 11-14 (user, after paper check 13's plate events needed rule after rule): the face's back meets the plate by the shared SPRING contact law at the
   shape's points (polygon corners, round rim as points), in both face_contact modes; no plate impacts, no held points, no series rule. The text below is superseded.
   (old) Pinned: the carried face reaching the plate, or an impact whose face would reach the plate within the impact itself (face travel over the impact's contact time
   more than the distance left: the two contacts overlap). The face stops (its energy to the plate); the ball meets face and plate as two contacts in series with the
   face massless between them (Hertz + KK each; the split of the squash is one internal value). Ends free when that contact opens.
e. Face alone on the plate: the BodyStop impact law, unchanged.
Evidence (paper, same laws as a reference with every contact a spring and both bodies with mass): head-on 18 hits within 3.7 % of return; 2-D with friction 16 hits,
exit angle within 3.1 deg; two balls within about 5 %; 3-D 20 hits, twist within 0.1 deg, lift within 3.2 deg, speed 0.1-6.8 % low (worst: light blade off-centre at
3 m/s, sensitive to the assumed twist inertia: a bench case). Energy never above the reference. 3-16 events per hit.


3. CARRIED CONSTRAINT IN THE GROUP STEP (shared engine piece)
--------------------------------------------------------------
a. One Lagrange multiplier lambda per carried pair, a new unknown with its row G = 0 (the ball-face gap closed at the step's end); lambda times the gradient of G in
   the ball's and the part's rows. SolveLinear pivots, so the zero diagonal is taken as it is. Rejected: eliminating the part's coordinate through the ball (no clean
   two-ball case).
b. Energy: the constraint does no work by construction (G = 0 at both ends; lambda times the discrete gradient of G gives lambda (G_end - G_start) = 0).
c. Friction uses lambda (the step's mean force, as every force enters the midpoint rows); the contact radius from Hertz a(lambda).
d. Events through the stop search: the step ends where a ball meets a free face (impact and rest rule between steps, as BodyStop impacts); a carried pair ends a step
   where its contact force at the step's END state reaches zero (not lambda: lambda is the step's mean, which released a step late with the face pulling, -4.24 N).
e. The multiplier rows join the group's convergence tests (else short steps accepted lambda unsolved).
f. Two balls: two multipliers, unique (balls with mass), each released on its own.
g. Step rule: the carried pair states its support stiffness with mass m + m_f, and the stiffening ahead (the foam's densification), as a steep field does (3d).
Evidence: offline prototype on the engine's own group step (runs/2026-11-14-carried-proto/numbers.txt): 2-5 Newton rounds, no failed solve, no energy step, halvings
recover, energy rise per step at most 1e-14 J, two balls fine.


4. SWITCH AND EVENTS (Requirements G5, G9)
------------------------------------------
Closes when the face's travel reaches x_close (from the contact gap at the blades through the lever, 2.2-3.0 mm at the face ESTIMATED), opens when the travel returns
below the same point (B rides with A once touched, so they part where they met: no hysteresis constant); B + C stiffness steps in at closing. Events into the part's event list after the step: on closing Hit and SwitchClosed, on opening Unhit and SwitchOpened;
HasHitEvent off drops Hit / Unhit only. CurrentAnimOffset reads the real travel.


5. SETTINGS ([Targets] for all, [Target:<name>] per target; every value's comment says ESTIMATED or TUNED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
plate = 1/0 (0: helper blade); pad = 1/0 (needs plate); plate_gap_mm 6.35 (fat 3.2); foam_k 600; foam_stiffen_mm 1.0; foam_stiffen_k 7.88e7; foam_return 0.80;
blade_thickness_mm, blade_width_mm, blade_length_mm, blade_material steel/brass (travel, twist, tilt); switch_gap_mm 1.2; switch_bc_k 900; helper_k; blade_loss 0.05 (TUNED: ring-down within 1 s).
face_contact = impact / spring: the ball-face contact by the impact law (sections 2-3, default) or the shared spring contact law (the engine's existing path). Both kept
permanently (user 11-14: spring for when its cost is solved); an agreed exception to DevelopmentRules 6's deletion of the old path. The bench compares the two on the
same hits (speed, angles, switch, energy, cost per cycle). The plate meets the face's back by the shared spring contact law at the shape's points in both.
Face friction: the target's own Friction. The model is behind its own ini setting, on by default while tested (DevelopmentRules 6).


6. ENERGY (ModelStandards 2h)
-----------------------------
Enters only through gravity on the ball (and nudge). The target has no drive. Stored: blade, B + C, foam (potentials). Lost: impacts (KK), rest joins, foam
Hunt-Crossley, the blade stack's loss, plate impacts, friction, the series contact's damping when pinned. The constraint does no work (3b). Checked per step by the group's book.


7. COST (estimate; DevelopmentRules 5: 30 us per cycle with the ball on it)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
No stiff spring in the motion: paper 3-16 events per hit, each ending a step (plus one stop-search step per release, prototype); prototype 16-25 steps per 3 m/s hit
at 2-5 rounds; a resting ball 1 round per cycle. INFERRED within the limit; measured with 1, 2 and 6 balls once built (ModelStandards 4b).


8. WHAT IT DOES NOT HANDLE OR MAY BREAK (DevelopmentRules 7; INFERRED unless stated)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- BUILD STATE 11-14 (uncommitted, unified-physics-branch; runs/2026-11-14-standup-build1, -build2, -build3, each results.md):
  piece 1 face_contact = spring: runs clean, worst cycle 14-24 ms (steps median 0.87 us, below the rule's omegaH / omega of about 4.7 us: cause not traced).
  piece 2 face_contact = impact: steps per hit about 100, return within 2-3 % of spring; faults: stop-search loop (fixed in piece 3, patch in build3, not applied),
  Newton stall on the twist row (Jacobian slope 3x the real one, a ball coupling missing; not found), energy gain from the carried constraint pushing out a ball
  that overlapped the face by rounding (touch stop must watch touching balls), friction while carried not built.
  Standards review open: build3/standards_review.md (epsilons as physics, silent limits kMaxBalls 16 / kMaxCarried 4 to remove, loss >= 0 check, [Target:] key
  report, stockcount.cpp). User to decide: settings switch_lever, mass_g, pad_grid (outside section 5).
- Two balls carried with the face at the plate: not modelled on paper.
- A ball held by a kicker is never carried (its motion is given).
- A ball pinched between a carried face and a wall: the wall's compliance takes it (reasoned, not tested).
- Carried flag and lambda's guess are ball state (snapshot self-test, trap 111); part switched off or table restart releases every carried ball.
- A ball starting inside the face: the overlap rule (no energy gained), never carried.
- Values: plastic restitution (0.5 at 0.5 m/s, a guess carried from the bumper), face-plate contact radius 5 mm, twist inertia: ASSUMED; a measured pad and face would replace them.
- Bench cases kept: light blade off-centre 3 m/s; no-pad plate hits at 3 m/s (paper -3.5 to -6.2 %).
