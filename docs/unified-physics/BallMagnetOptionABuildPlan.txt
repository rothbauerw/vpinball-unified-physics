Source of decisions: to-do list entry "OPTION A BUILD SPEC" (s23). Base code: commit c7921fc7f (src/physics/magnetballs.cpp/.h and its magnet.cpp hooks).
Speed-ups: runs/2026-10-13-cpp-proto (bench_recipe_opt.cpp, bench_recipe_fast.cpp, lean_fast.h). Offline reference: runs/2026-10-14-mirror (test_magnetballs.cpp, optA_*.py).

BUILD STEPS (each one ends with a check before the next starts)
B1. Restore magnetballs.cpp/.h from c7921fc7f, add them to the build, hook them into magnet.cpp UpdateBallInteraction only when ContactSystem::Unified() and the
    magnet is core_model = cells with ball_interaction on. No ball_model key (the old one stays a notice). Check: build; offline test_magnetballs.cpp reproduces
    the c7921fc7f forces on its saved cases (same code, same answer).
B2. Pair fix range (spec 1). Pair operators built for surface gaps 0.1 .. 12 mm (today 0.1 .. 1 mm, 6 gaps; new ~12 gaps, log-spaced), once per process at load,
    current-independent. The 1 mm TOUCH limit and the 0.1 m REACH go: every pair of gated balls within 12 mm gets the pair fix and order-8 fields; all other gated
    pairs are in the order-4 global solve only. The saturation tree (cut rule R1) uses the same pairs; its factor is 1 beyond 3 mm by the FEM table, so far pairs
    change nothing there. Check: build time of the operators logged (0.3 s INFERRED); offline forces vs the Python pair-fix-vs-gap cases (pairfix_gap.json, 0.1-6 mm).
B3. Magnetisation gate (spec 2, flux metric, user s24), replaces REACH 0.127 m. A ball is gated when (a) the magnets' field at its centre at the PRESENT current is
    >= 0.665 mT (two touching balls in that field pull ~8 mN, REPORT.md "GATE"), or (b) its surface is within half a ball diameter of a gated ball AND the field at
    its centre from the magnets plus the gated balls (last cycle's solve) is >= 0.665 mT; (b) is applied until no ball is added. Non-magnetic and kicker-locked
    balls never join. Check: offline, gate members on the chain and pile cases.
B4. Magnet's own frame (spec 4). Each magnet's drive is looked up in its own frame (axis = the magnet axis, origin = face centre), rotated to the table frame
    (Wigner rotation, skipped when the axis is +z) and summed per ball before the solve. The axis_z < 0.999 refusal goes. No rotated-frame test (user s24);
    requirement instead: EVERY magnet-dependent part works in the magnet's frame - drive lookup, plug pick (offset and gap from the face along the axis), face
    saturation factor, gate (a), the lone-ball pull, and the re-aim's magnet part (B7). Check: code review of each of these against the frame.
B5. Iterations and warm start (spec 6). Order-4 global solve: 3 Gauss-Seidel rounds from last cycle's answer (today: up to 100 sweeps to 1e-12, warm only when the
    ball count is unchanged). Pair feedback: 2 passes from last cycle's corrected coefficients (today: 3 passes from cold). Warm state kept per ball by a stable ball
    id, not by array position; a ball new to the solve starts from its own response (-beta b). Reset at table start. Check: offline, the warm-pass and GS cases of
    optA_warm.py / optA_gs.py (errors there: 2.3 % worst at 3 mm/cycle into contact with GS 3, 0.31 % with 2 passes).
B6. Bench speed-ups (spec 7): partner field reused from the field step; pair force form on the last pass only; rotations as row phases x real small-d; small-d
    terms precomputed at construction. Each port checked against the step before it on the same cases (bench: results equal to 13 digits). Check: cost of the
    engine code itself on the 6-ball pile, one core, worst of repeated runs (~300 us INFERRED; budget 200; user accepts, play testing decides).
B7. Re-aim per substep (spec 3). The solve runs once per 1 ms cycle; its result is split into
      - per pair: the pair force, stored in the pair's frame (along / across the line of centres) and turned with the CURRENT line of centres at every substep;
      - per ball: the rest (the change of the ball's own pull from the magnet), stored in the magnet's frame at the ball (radial / round / along the axis) and
        re-expressed at the ball's current position round the axis at every substep (it follows the drive).
    The split is exact at the start of the cycle (the parts add up to the solve's answer, saturation included). The contact system applies it in Sources::Add
    next to the lone-ball pull; balls outside the contact system keep the held per-cycle increment (m_magPair). The substep bound uses the real magnetic
    acceleration |F| / m instead of the fixed 60 m/s2 (Option A forces reach ~20 N on an 80 g ball). Check: offline, a ball circling its partner at constant gap gets
    no work from the magnet over many cycles; engine energy test (below).
B8. Everything behind VPX_UNIFIED (spec 5): DONE as its own step 2026-10-20 (PhysicsIni::Active; unified on by default, 0 = pure stock).
B9. Diagnostics (rule 8: on-screen notice + WARN, once per cause per table start): cost over budget (worst cycle, with ball count), plug ball off the face centre,
    ball of another size, more than 6 gated balls, two balls on one face, a ring of touching balls (extra contact ignored by the tree). Timing csv: mag_us exists;
    add gated balls, pair-fix pairs, GS rounds. Log for the user's captures: VPX_MAG_LOG (per cycle: gated balls, pairs, per-ball force parts, cost), replacing
    VPX_NET_LOG in "tables\VPX play - magnet log.bat".
B10. Docs: NewPhysicsReference (MAGNETS section + dated change log), to-do list, this plan's results.

WHAT IT DOES NOT HANDLE OR MAY BREAK (rule 7)
Already accepted (spec, no discussion needed): plug off-centre, no ball on the face, two balls on one face, rings, core not answering neighbours, tables for one
  core, repulsion case of the pair fix, several magnets, air-core, other ball sizes, more than 6 balls.
New from this plan:
 1. Gate (b) uses last cycle's force: a ball joins one cycle late (step <= 8 mN by the gate's definition). REASONED.
 2. A ball joining the solve starts cold: the 3-round GS error for a cold ball is NOT measured (warm cases only). Handled: it starts from its own response; to measure in T1.
 3. Pair entering the 12 mm range: the fix switches on there. Measured fix at 6 mm: 31-41 mN on the plug ball (c19 NI 6200); at 12 mm under 8 mN is INFERRED
    (extrapolated). To measure in T1 (one case at 12 mm).
 4. Balls removed mid-game (drain, kicker lock, script delete) or a second Play in the editor: warm state keyed by ball id and reset at table start. To check in T3/T4.
 5. Substep bound change (B7) changes the substep count of every magnetised ball, so the contact-system cost moves too. Measured in T2/T4 timing (mag_us and step_us).
 6. A tilted magnet (B4) is checked only against a rotated vertical one; no FEM for a tilted case.
 7. The re-aim split (B7) holds the size of each part for the cycle; fast gap changes inside one cycle (1-3 mm per cycle) are only seen at the next solve. The
    T1 warm cases show the per-cycle error; within the cycle it is NOT measured.
 8. Legacy engine and air-core magnets: unchanged (dipoles), so their known errors stay.
