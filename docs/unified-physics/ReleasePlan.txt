STOCK VPX PHYSICS CHANGES -- SOURCE REVIEW, 2026-10-21
====================================================

Scope and evidence
------------------
New stock source: unified-physics-branch committed HEAD da8b594aed731d905c09a94ddb76100dbd4ebedb,
2026-10-21. The working directory also contains uncommitted unified-model changes;
those were EXCLUDED by inspecting git objects, not by diffing working files.
Previous unified-model stock base: b003609ae7304fa8d7f4781dfec08672a5894ed6,
2026-09-18 (also the current vpinball checkout).
September audit/review baseline: master-src fb0531451, 2026-09-21.
The main solver changes below are after the September 21 baseline. Between
September 18 and 21 there were also nudge sensor defaults/settings fixes,
including default sensor cabinet mass changing from 1 kg to 113 kg.

This is a source/history analysis. Runtime effects below are INFERRED from the
equations and control flow, not newly measured gameplay results. No build,
timing run, regression matrix, engine edit, commit or push was performed.
Commit messages describing prior test results are historical evidence only.

Main conclusion
---------------
Stock is materially different from the September engine. Many earlier audit
issues now have upstream changes. It is still the impulse/contact engine,
not the project's compliant unified contact model.

In stock src/physics/physconst.h, FIX_PHYSICS is defined and NEW_PHYSICS is
commented out. These are compile-time choices, not a table-facing model switch.
The active fixes are not gated here by table-file version. Some further fixes
are unconditional and remain even if FIX_PHYSICS is undefined.

Active changes and likely effects
--------------------------------
1. WALL/SURFACE CONTACT FRICTION
   49add616c (Oct 1): limits the old low-normal-speed static-friction shortcut
   to support-like normals (z > 0.5). Sliding on vertical walls now reaches
   slip-directed dynamic friction.
   3904ce93b (Oct 1): contact friction is bounded by the normal impulse actually
   applied in that step, rather than only gravity projected onto the surface.
   8c4cf05d3 (Oct 4): dynamic sliding asks for the full slip-removing impulse,
   bounded by Coulomb friction, rather than multiplying it by time again.
   Same friction values can therefore produce different wall riding, spin
   and sliding. The support-normal shortcut itself has not been removed.
   Sources: hitball.cpp, HandleStaticContact and ApplyFriction.

2. CONTACT DETECTION ON WALLS, POLYGONS AND PLAYFIELD
   61ed4a5ad (Oct 2): slow shallow touches now report immediate contact instead
   of artificial hit times that could lie outside the simulation step.
   8c4cf05d3: slow balls too deeply embedded in the playfield take the collision
   path rather than being ignored. Polygon contact handling is active under FIX.
   Likely affects settling, slow wall touches and playfield penetration.
   Sources: collide.cpp LineSeg::HitTestBasic; collideex.cpp Hit3DPoly/HitPlane.

3. BALL MASS
   93953469f (Oct 1): corrects mass handling in support/contact response and
   removes division by mass from gravity acceleration at the ball surface.
   Non-unit-mass balls should no longer gain different gravity acceleration
   or be incorrectly lifted by their resting support.
   Source: hitball.cpp HandleStaticContact and SurfaceAcceleration.

4. BALL-TO-BALL CONTACT AND STACKS
   70ff6c6be (Sep 26): evaluates the collision normal using both balls advanced
   to the hit time. This corrects the sign/time construction of the normal.
   eb9ae26c3 (Oct 2): enables ball-ball contacts; the old BALL_CONTACTS switch
   is removed. Slow touching pairs report immediate events.
   f4d8b8cd7 (Oct 2): stabilizes resting stacks. Shallow resting-scale impacts
   skip the large positional correction; small overlap gets a capped position
   correction. Ball-ball restitution is now 0 below the low-impact threshold
   and remains 0.8 above it, instead of always being 0.8.
   8c4cf05d3: contact slip uses relative surface velocity of the two balls;
   the contact friction coefficient becomes 0.1 instead of inherited 0.3.
   Ball-ball contacts run before other contacts; later supports compensate
   earlier contact changes. The cancellation is limited so a fresh impact can
   reach the next ball rather than disappearing into a resting contact.
   Likely affects ball locks, packed troughs, stacked balls and cradle-like
   ball chains. This is a sequential contact solve, not a coupled island solver.
   Sources: hitball.cpp HitTest/Collide/Contact/HandleStaticContact/ApplyFriction;
   PhysicsEngine.cpp contact passes.

5. FLIPPER INERTIA AND CONTACT
   887d368b8 (Sep 26): corrects the sign of the flipper inertia contribution
   in the static-friction denominator.
   a75d4da2d (Oct 2): replaces rod inertia with shape-based inertia under FIX.
   The shape is a uniform-density convex hull of the base and end circles.
   m_backwards_compatibility is hardcoded true; torque and torque ramp rate
   are rescaled to preserve free-swing acceleration. This does NOT preserve
   collision response: dead bounces, held-bat response and ball launch can change.
   8c4cf05d3: tests contact-gap acceleration using the ball center, removing
   the spin-induced centripetal term that could incorrectly remove support.
   Also preserves angular speed when updating inertia and guards degenerate
   inertia results. 6594db978 (Oct 5) preserves fractional rubber dimensions
   when loading packs, so the loaded geometry can differ from older builds.
   Sources: hitflipper.cpp UpdateInertia/Contact; parts/flipper.cpp Load.

6. RAMPS AND WALL GEOMETRY
   dd0b67829 (Sep 30): removes a duplicate ramp entrance joint.
   4cd61d6ba (Oct 14): safer zero-length/degenerate ramp handling and resets
   triangle adjacency across degenerate triangles.
   4c0cea03c (Oct 14): clockwise/reversed walls collide on both sides and
   keep their slingshot animation.
   These change collision construction, not the general material model.
   Sources: parts/ramp.cpp PhysicSetup/CheckJoint; parts/surface.cpp.

7. COLLISION SEARCH RELIABILITY
   543518a3a (Oct 1): KD leaf scan stays within the node's item range.
   8c4cf05d3 and ce1feb440: SSE lane-mask changes avoid duplicate hit tests
   and contacts; async tree/threading fixes address construction races.
   af0c4a5b4 (Sep 24) introduces lazy hit-quadtree updates; later pool fixes
   provide fallback work when a queue is full. These matter when evaluating
   contact counts and runtime geometry, even without a new force law.

8. NUDGE
   d75ba2dd4 (Sep 24): cabinet oscillator X/Y frequencies change from
   9.3/5.8 Hz to 4.5/4.3 Hz; X damping changes from 0.052 to 0.04,
   Y stays 0.055. Displacement multipliers 3.5/2.0 are removed.
   Later changes use a shared strong-nudge acceleration of 0.7 g instead
   of the prior 0.5 g scale for the cabinet-model keyboard/gamepad paths.
   There are initialization and sensor-type scale-conversion fixes too.
   These are specific input/model paths; do not apply them to every nudge mode.
   Sources: physics/cabinet; shared constant in cabinet/NudgeHandler.h.

9. PLUNGER, INPUT AND ENGINE LIFECYCLE
   89e4310d2/5dbcc0351 (Sep 25-26): plunger unit corrections and a mechanical
   release path that drives the simulated tip at estimated launch speed,
   avoiding missed/weak launches with low Mech Strength.
   d7c565580 (Oct 7): guards degenerate plunger-tip normals.
   Engine refactoring lets the real solver run without the global player
   for integration tests; c4078b887 prevents simulation before engine start.
   Haptic changes alter rumble aggregation/dispatch, not the collision law.

Present but disabled in the stock source
---------------------------------------
NEW_PHYSICS contains contact-patch spin friction, rolling resistance, friction
and spin exchange during ball-ball IMPACTS, revised embedded-ball push-out and
separation, and broader embedded-contact handling. Its constants are explicitly
described as uncalibrated.

687df4f61/43dbc3ff9 (Oct 10/12) are important compatibility follow-ups:
Collide3DWall keeps the legacy PRE-restitution friction bound in default stock.
The larger post-restitution bound is confined to NEW_PHYSICS. Do not read
3904ce93b alone and conclude all wall bounce friction uses the larger bound.

Still present / not resolved by these changes
--------------------------------------------
- The legacy resting-ball spin hack and embedding/displacement workarounds
  remain enabled because NEW_PHYSICS is off.
- The parked-flipper stop/recoil heuristic remains, including the 0.5 response
  scaling. Shape inertia is not the earlier proposed stop fix.
- Ramp sidewalls still use 2D segments with a single lower/upper height per
  segment. AddWallLineSeg still documents extension below/above the ramp.
  The ramp entrance-joint fix is not the earlier ramp sidewall fix.
- Gates/spinners retain their kinematic collision shortcut; source comments
  explicitly discuss keeping the energy-exchanging upgrade opt-in.
- Slingshot force is not rebuilt; elasticity dependence remains a known test.
- Static contact compensation intentionally leaves other static NORMAL
  impulses independent (wire ramps/corners); it is not a full simultaneous solve.

Tests and limits
----------------
Stock now includes a PhysicsEngine integration harness and physics defect,
spin, collision, quadtree, cabinet and related tests. The Oct 4 commit reports
75/75 physics tests for that revision. That is not a fresh result for Oct 21.
Several tests explicitly use doctest::should_fail(), including vertical polygon
hits, embedded resting balls, slingshot elasticity dependence, non-legacy kicker
entry and flipper end-cap support. Passing the suite does not establish that
these known defects are fixed. No tests were run in this source review.

Implication for the unified branch (INFERRED)
-------------------------------------------
Use da8b594ae as the new stock reference when judging fallback/disabled-model
behavior. Old results against b003609ae/fb0531451 are historical comparisons,
not measurements of today's fallback. The highest-priority overlap checks
are stock ApplyFriction, static contact handling, ball-pair handling, flipper
inertia/torque scaling and contact ordering. Confirm which path owns each
response before retaining older local fixes; otherwise upstream and local
corrections can overlap. No conclusion about actual double application in
the current uncommitted port is made here; that requires a separate port audit.
